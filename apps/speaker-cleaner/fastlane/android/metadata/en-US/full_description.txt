HOW TO REMOVE WATER FROM A PHONE SPEAKER
1. Remove anything covering the speaker and place the device speaker-side down.
2. Start at a low, comfortable volume and disconnect headphones or external speakers.
3. Run a guided cleaning cycle.
4. Use the speaker test to check the result, then repeat if the sound is still muffled.

Sound waves may help move trapped droplets and loosen light dust without opening your phone. Speaker Cleaner is useful after rain, a splash, or other light moisture exposure, but it cannot repair hardware damage.
