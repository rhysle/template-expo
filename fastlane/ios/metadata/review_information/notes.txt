3) PURPOSE & AUDIENCE
Speaker Cleaner guides users through speaker-cleaning sound cycles, tone generation, stereo-channel checks, and nearby sound-level estimates.
It is for people checking a muffled phone after moisture/debris exposure, testing headphones, or monitoring noise without switching apps.
Unlike typical single-tone eject apps, Speaker Cleaner shows a three-stage water-pulse, debris-shake, and finishing-sweep workflow followed by tools for checking the result.
Shipped features include 30/60/90-second cycles, 20-20,000 Hz tones with four waveforms, manual/automatic left-right-both stereo testing, and live estimated dB with session minimum/average/maximum.

4) SETUP
No account or demo credentials are required.
Complete or skip onboarding and dismiss the paywall; Eject, Tone Generator, Stereo Test, and dB Meter are in the tab bar.
Open Premium by starting cleaning, choosing an advanced waveform, enabling automatic stereo testing, viewing detailed dB statistics, or tapping Upgrade in Settings.
Subscriptions and lifetime access use Apple In-App Purchase; RevenueCat manages offerings and the Premium entitlement, and no external payment is offered.
Restore Purchases is on the paywall.

5) EXTERNAL SERVICES

Privacy: microphone samples stay in device memory and are never saved/uploaded. A locally stored anonymous install UUID is shared with Firebase, Sentry, and RevenueCat; AdMob follows consent/tracking choices.
